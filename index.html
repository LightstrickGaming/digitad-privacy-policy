Privacy Policy
Lightstrick Gaming

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Thank you for playing Digitad, developed by Lightstrick Gaming.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use Digitad, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

Digitad is an offline puzzle game and does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to play the game.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Digitad does not require users to provide personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • User account information
  • Password
  • Payment information

Information Collected Automatically

When rewarded advertisements are displayed, our advertising service provider may automatically process certain technical information necessary to provide, measure, and protect advertising services.

Depending on the advertising services and applicable privacy settings, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device type and operating system
  • Language and regional settings
  • Approximate location such as country or region
  • IP address
  • Advertising performance and interaction information

This information is processed by the relevant advertising service providers and is not directly collected by Lightstrick Gaming for its own database.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by advertising service providers may be used to:

  • Deliver rewarded advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or invalid advertising activity
  • Maintain and improve advertising services

Lightstrick Gaming does not sell personal information.

4. Rewarded Advertisements

Digitad uses Unity LevelPlay to provide rewarded advertisements. Players may voluntarily choose to watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for an optional in-game reward.

Watching rewarded advertisements is optional and is not required to play the core game.

Unity LevelPlay and advertising partners participating in its advertising services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

5. Local Game Data

Digitad stores certain gameplay preferences locally on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs.

This may include information such as:

  • Selected game mode
  • Audio settings
  • Other local gameplay preferences

Digitad does not transmit this locally stored gameplay data to Lightstrick Gaming servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Digitad is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children.

Digitad does not provide account creation or a mechanism for players to directly submit personal information to us.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through Digitad, please contact us using the information below.

7. Third-Party Services

Digitad uses the following third-party service:

  • Unity LevelPlay — used to provide rewarded advertising services.

Third-party services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of these providers for more information about their data practices.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information under our control.

Because Digitad does not maintain user accounts or operate its own servers for storing personal player information, the amount of personal information directly controlled by Lightstrick Gaming is limited.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region, you may have certain rights regarding personal information processed by third-party advertising providers. These rights may include requesting access to, correction or deletion of information, or objecting to certain forms of processing.

Some privacy choices may also be available through your device settings or through mechanisms provided by advertising providers.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Digitad, our services, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the Effective Date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or Digitad's privacy practices, please contact us.
